# Voxel Shell 80 mm enclosure

The [Voxel Shell](../voxel-shell/README.md) sized for flexible **80 × 80 mm WS2812 8×8 panels** (10 mm pitch) instead of the rigid 65 × 66 mm boards. The cube is still 8×8×8 and the firmware is unchanged.

The design is the same as the 65 mm version:

- six mitred, translucent face shells;
- funnel light cells under a 1.0 mm diffuser skin;
- every wall at least 1.0 mm;
- all 12 edges bevelled;
- the USB-C socket and SS12F15 switch on the bottom front bevel.

The result is a **103.6 mm** cube with a 94 mm lit area on each face.

Every STL here is generated by `../voxel-shell/generate.py` with `VOXEL_PROFILE=80mm`, and all fit and printability checks pass.

View it in 3D: <https://psytraxx.github.io/gravity-cube-rs/voxel-shell-80mm/viewer.html>

Each shell is 100 × 100 × 10.3 mm and uses about 36 cm³ of resin.

## The panel needs a backing sheet

A flexible panel will not stay flat in its pocket. Glue each panel to an **80 × 80 × 1 mm backing sheet**. Aluminium also spreads the LED heat; FR4 or acrylic work too. Use thin double-sided tape or a thin layer of glue.

The pocket is designed for a **1.6 mm stack**: flexible PCB (about 0.3–0.5 mm), adhesive and the 1 mm sheet.

- If your stack is thinner, shim it.
- If it is thicker, change `PCB_T` in the `80mm` block of `generate.py` and rebuild.

The pocket has 0.4 mm clearance on each side, so an 80.7 mm sheet still fits.

A 0.6 mm ring of the shell rests on the panel margin. The outermost light cells are pulled 0.5 mm in from the panel edge to make room for it. Outer LEDs still have 1.2 mm clearance; inner LEDs have 1.7 mm.

The connectors and power leads on the back of the panel go into the cube interior. The open channel along every edge carries wires between faces.

## Rebuilding

```bash
cd case/voxel-shell
pip install manifold3d trimesh numpy rtree
VOXEL_PROFILE=80mm python3 generate.py    # writes into case/voxel-shell-80mm/
VOXEL_PROFILE=80mm python3 check_fit.py   # must end with "All checks passed."
```
