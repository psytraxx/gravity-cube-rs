Gravity Cube frame concepts

Five frame designs for six 65 × 65 mm WS2812 8×8 panels together as a cube. Each one is drawn to scale in millimetres. Drag to turn the cube. The lit "water" always settles toward the bottom, just like the running simulation. The bottom front edge has the USB-C port and power switch.

USB-C port and power switch

WhereThe six panels close the cube on every side, so the port can't go through a face. Each concept puts a USB-C socket (about 9 × 3.3 mm) and a small slide switch in a frame "pod" on the bottom front edge, drawn in the viewer.
Wiring inFour thin wires (28–30 AWG: 5 V, GND, and the battery through the switch) run from the pod through one of the panel's roughly 2 mm mounting holes. The ESP32 and battery can go in later without touching the frame.
AlternativeIn the Lantern Cage, the panels can sit 3.5 mm off the core. That opens a channel under every rail, so the socket board fits inside the rail and no holes are needed.
