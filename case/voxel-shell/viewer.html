Voxel Shell

This shows the actual STL files: six mitred face shells in translucent resin, each holding one 65 × 66 mm WS2812 panel. The light cells fan out from the LEDs so the pixels cover 80 mm of each 89.6 mm face. Drag to turn the cube and use the slider to pull it apart. The clear USB-C socket and green slide switch sit on the 45° bevel of the bottom front edge; every edge of the cube is bevelled.

Parts to buy

1 ×SS12F15 slide switch (19.6 × 5.5 plate)
2 ×M2 × 4 screw + nut for the switch, or glue it
24 ×2 mm dowel, about 5.5 mm long (1.75 mm filament works)

Assembly

  1. Press each panel into the pocket of its shell, LEDs facing the grid. A few dots of glue on the PCB rim hold it.
  2. Fit the USB-C socket from outside and the switch from inside the front port shell, then run their leads into the cube.
  3. Wire the panels, ESP32 and battery inside. The open channel along every edge carries the wires between faces.
  4. Put dowels in the mitre holes and close the cube. Glue the last face, or leave it dry-fitted so you can open it again.
