# Voxel Shell 16×16 enclosure

The Voxel Shell scaled up for six flexible 160 × 160 mm WS2812 16×16 panels
(10 mm pitch, 256 LEDs each, 1536 LEDs in total). The design is the same as the
[8×8 Voxel Shell](../voxel-shell/README.md):

- six mitred, translucent face shells with funnel light cells;
- a 1.0 mm diffuser skin and every wall at least 1.0 mm;
- all 12 edges bevelled;
- a USB-C socket and SS12F15 switch on the bottom front bevel.

The result is a **183.6 mm** cube with a 174 mm lit area on each face.

Every STL here is generated by `../voxel-shell/generate.py` with
`VOXEL_PROFILE=16x16`, and all fit and printability checks pass.

View it in 3D: <https://psytraxx.github.io/gravity-cube-rs/voxel-shell-16x16/viewer.html>

Each shell is 180 × 180 × 10.3 mm and uses about 108 cm³ of resin, roughly 0.65 l for all six. Check that the print service's build volume takes 180 mm.

## The panel needs a backing sheet

A flexible panel will not stay flat in a 160 mm pocket. Glue each panel to a
**160 × 160 × 1 mm backing sheet**. Aluminium also spreads the LED heat; FR4 or
acrylic work too. Use thin double-sided tape or a thin layer of glue.

The shell's pocket is designed for a **1.6 mm stack**: flexible PCB (about 0.3–0.5 mm), adhesive and the 1 mm sheet.

- If your stack is thinner, shim it.
- If it is thicker, change `PCB_T` in the `16x16` block of `generate.py` and rebuild.

The pocket has 0.4 mm clearance on each side, so a 160.7 mm sheet still fits. A
0.6 mm ring of the shell rests on the panel margin, and the outermost light cells
are pulled 0.5 mm in from the panel edge to make room for it. The outer LEDs still
have 1.2 mm clearance; inner LEDs have 1.7 mm.

The connector and power leads on the back of the panel go into the cube
interior. The open channel along every edge carries wires between faces.

## Power

1536 WS2812 LEDs draw up to about **90 A at 5 V** at full white. A single USB-C
socket cannot supply that.

- Cap the global brightness in firmware: about 10–15 % keeps a typical animation
  near 5–10 A.
- Use a supply sized for the cap you choose.
- Feed power into every panel through its own voltage wires, not only through the data chain.

## Firmware

The simulation and firmware are still set up for an 8×8×8 cube. A 16×16 face
needs `cube_size: 16` and a 16×16 panel mapper.

## Rebuilding

```bash
cd case/voxel-shell
pip install manifold3d trimesh numpy rtree
VOXEL_PROFILE=16x16 python3 generate.py    # writes into case/voxel-shell-16x16/
VOXEL_PROFILE=16x16 python3 check_fit.py   # must end with "All checks passed."
```
